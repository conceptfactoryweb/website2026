About — The Who

Our storyBuilt in-house since day one

Concept Factory is a Belgian creative production studio for themed environments, immersive experiences and scenic decor — designing and building the impossible from a single 2,250 m² atelier in Herentals.

(01)

When the brief sounds impossible, Concept Factory is the call.

From the first sketch to the final reveal, every discipline lives under one roof — concept, technical design, fabrication, scenic paint and on-site installation. No middlemen, no compromise on craft, full responsibility from A to Z.
